# Source and model redistribution review

Checked 24 November 2026. The inventory links to source-scoped terms in `data/source_expansion/cohort.json`; the original manifest remains the collection record. Preserve originals and provenance for the approved local review environment. Before any distribution, check the terms for each exact file and its derived outputs. Treat the statuses below as evidence of the terms located, with the remaining permissions recorded explicitly.

| Material | Terms located and required action |
| --- | --- |
| MP CM Helpline scheme text, S06 | The portal allows accurate reproduction of its material with prominent source acknowledgment, and excludes third-party material. Apply that condition to the portal-owned scheme text. Omit site chrome, logos and third-party linked content unless their terms are separately verified. [Publisher copyright policy](https://cmhelpline.mp.gov.in/allpolicies.aspx) |
| Government OGD datasets | GODL permits reuse of resources actually released under it, subject to attribution and exclusions. Record catalog title, publisher, resource ID, URL and retrieval date. The DES-origin mirror and standalone DES report PDFs lack evidence of exact identity with a licensed bulk resource. Keep the mirror and PDFs in local review pending a licensed export or specific permission. [GODL text](https://data.gov.in/Godl) · [DES query report](https://data.desagri.gov.in/website/apy-query-report-web) |
| Market-board pages, e-NAM, Economic Survey, IMD bulletin, ICAR, DG/Ministry documents, PMFBY and NCCD | Record publisher and exact URL from the manifest and cohort. Retrieve the terms associated with each exact resource. Existing public access, copyright notices and privacy policies do not establish an open data grant. Honor the MP Agricultural Engineering policy and obtain any reproduction permission it requires. Keep unresolved snapshots and linked assets in the course review environment. [e-NAM policy](https://enam.gov.in/Privacy-Policy) · [Source-scoped records](../data/source_expansion/cohort.json) |
| Historical Agmarknet archive, training examples and evaluation slices | The files are repackaged DMI-origin records. Confirm upstream rights for the pinned historical archives and derivative redistribution. A repository with public access alone does not document those rights. Keep original hashes, dates, source references and date separation for review. [Agmarknet publisher](https://www.agmarknet.gov.in/) · [Verifiable cases](../eval/m18_cases.json) |
| Historical district GeoJSON, maps and GIF | The exact ArcGIS layer identifies Survey of India copyright. Official guidelines identify SoI boundaries as the standard and allow digital display and printing of SoI boundary data. Exact layer redistribution terms remain unresolved. Retain publisher attribution, show the historical vintage, and obtain the exact layer's redistribution terms or use a compatible direct SoI product with documented terms before distributing raw boundaries or derived map bundles. [Exact layer](https://services2.arcgis.com/fQDJ6zNJPJ0yhFjv/ArcGIS/rest/services/IND_AdminBoundary/FeatureServer/2) · [SoI geospatial guidelines](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/GeospatialGuidelines.aspx) · [SoI download FAQ](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/FAQs.aspx) |
| LGD district registry | The owner downloaded the direct ministry workbook. The workbook, CSV and generated 55-district registry agree. Keep the workbook, conversion and registry local while confirming the terms for redistributing the exact export and generated registry. [Official catalog](https://www.data.gov.in/resource/local-government-directory-lgd-districts) · [Ministry directory](https://lgdirectory.gov.in/downloadDirectory.do) |
| Qwen base and trained PEFT adapter | The pinned base declares Apache 2.0. Retain its license and required attribution/notices when redistributing covered material. Separate the base license from the dataset, generated adapter and course IP/NDA review. Publish the adapter only after the team and course confirm the approved destination and rights, then put the URL and verified SHA-256 in the repository. [Pinned model license](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct/blob/989aa7980e4cf806f80c7fef2b1adb7bc71aa306/LICENSE) |

Keep farmer identifiers and beneficiary-level records out of the corpus and tracing. Retained directory records may include professional addresses. Check source excerpts and logs before sharing. Ask the TA which course-controlled location can host approved snapshots and weights. Ask each publisher for the exact permission where terms are unresolved, save the ruling and attribution with the manifest, and maintain a list of artifacts restricted to local review.
